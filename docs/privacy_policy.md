# RadioWave Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-22

RadioWave is an internet radio player that lets you discover public radio stations, play live streams, save favorites, and add custom stream URLs.

## Data We Store

RadioWave stores app preferences locally on your device, including favorites, custom station entries, playback state, theme choice, and equalizer settings. This data is not sent to RadioWave servers.

## Network Requests

RadioWave connects to the public Radio Browser API to load station and country lists. When you play a station, your device connects directly to that station's stream provider. Those third-party services may receive standard technical information such as your IP address and device request headers.

## Analytics and Advertising

RadioWave does not currently include analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or crash reporting SDKs.

## Children

RadioWave is a general-audience utility app and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
